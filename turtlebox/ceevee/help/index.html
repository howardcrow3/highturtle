CeeVee help

Installing CeeVee, getting started, your license and fixes for common problems.

Install

Windows 11: run CeeVee-setup.exe. It installs for your user account and needs no administrator password. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway, but only if you downloaded the file from turtlebox.studio.

Mac (Apple silicon): open CeeVee.dmg and drag CeeVee to Applications.

CeeVee updates itself. To check now, open Settings, then About CeeVee, and choose Check for updates.

First steps

  1. Open CeeVee. Your 14-day trial starts automatically.
  2. In Settings, Local AI, download the AI model. It is about 2.5 GB, downloads once and runs on your computer.
  3. On the Welcome page, paste your resume. CeeVee shows what it found; tick what to keep.
  4. In Settings, add the kinds of jobs you want under Job categories and the companies you like under Companies to watch.
  5. CeeVee searches in the background and shows new jobs under Jobs. It keeps running in the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac) when you close the window. Quit from there.

Buying and your license

  • CeeVee costs $19.99 once, and one license works on 2 computers. Buy it from Settings, License, or on this site.
  • Your key arrives by email. Click the activation link in the email, or paste the key into Settings, License.
  • To move to a new computer, open Settings, License and choose Remove from this computer, then activate on the new one.
  • After the trial without a license, CeeVee turns read-only. You can still look at and export everything.

Problems

  • The model will not download. Check your connection and that you have about 3 GB free, then try again. Downloads resume where they stopped.
  • CeeVee closed unexpectedly. It says so on the next start. Open Settings, About CeeVee, Open log folder, and send the files to info@turtlebox.studio.
  • No jobs appear. Add a job category and some companies in Settings, then wait for the next search.
  • The activation link does nothing. Paste the key into Settings, License instead.
  • CeeVee says the trial is over too early. CeeVee notices clock changes. Set your computer's clock to the right time.

Where your data is

Windows: %APPDATA%\studio.turtlebox.ceevee. Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/studio.turtlebox.ceevee. To back it up, copy that folder while CeeVee is closed.

Still stuck? Write to info@turtlebox.studio.
