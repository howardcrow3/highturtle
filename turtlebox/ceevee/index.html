What CeeVee does

Getting ready

  • Your profile, once

    Drop in your resume, your LinkedIn profile PDF or your LinkedIn data export. CeeVee builds your profile from it and shows what it found before saving anything.

  • Jobs that fit

    CeeVee searches for new jobs in the categories you pick while it runs in the menu bar or system tray, scores each one against your profile and tells you when something new turns up.

  • People you know

    Import your LinkedIn connections and CeeVee shows who you know at each company on your list.

Applying

  • A tailored resume for each job

    CeeVee lists what the job asks for, shows where you match and where you don't, and drafts a resume and cover letter. It never invents experience.

  • Edit in plain words

    Change a document yourself, or ask for a change such as “make the opening shorter”. Every version is kept, with undo and redo.

  • PDF and Word files

    Resumes and cover letters are saved as PDF and Word files in a normal folder on your computer.

  • Applications filled in for you

    CeeVee opens the application page in its own browser window, fills in the fields from your profile and attaches your files. It stops before Submit.

Following up

  • Nothing sent without you

    Anything that would go out in your name waits on one list for you to approve or skip.

  • Replies matched to applications

    Connect Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud or any mailbox. CeeVee links each reply from an employer to its application and updates the status.

  • Answers drafted for you

    For each reply, CeeVee drafts an answer for you to edit and send.

  • Yours to keep

    Export everything, or delete everything, any time. The AI model runs on your computer, so there is no AI account or key.
