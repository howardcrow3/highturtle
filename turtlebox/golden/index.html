What Golden does

Your books

  • Statements in, books out

    Import the CSV or OFX files from your bank and card accounts. Golden files each statement in a library so you can find it again.

  • Categories that learn

    Transactions are sorted into categories. Fix one and Golden remembers that merchant next time.

  • Budgets and cash flow

    Set a spending budget, and see what came in and went out by week, month, quarter or year.

  • Net worth

    Everything you own and owe on one balance sheet, with a 12-month forecast of where you're headed.

Your retirement

  • The month you can retire

    Golden projects your money forward with federal and state income tax, Social Security and pensions.

  • The chance it lasts

    Thousands of possible market outcomes show how likely your money is to last, and a good and a rough decade.

  • Ask “what if”

    Type a question such as “what if we both retire at 62” and see the plan with and without the change. Keep it or dismiss it.

  • Two people, one plan

    Every account and income belongs to one of you or to both, each with their own retirement and Social Security age.

Your data

  • Tax estimate

    Estimate this year's federal and state income tax, plus Social Security and Medicare tax on wages.

  • Encrypted on your computer

    The database is encrypted, and its key is kept in your computer's keychain. In Private mode nothing leaves your computer except license and update checks.

  • Export and back up

    Export transactions as CSV, and back up or restore everything in one file.

  • Read only, by design

    Golden never moves money or places trades.
