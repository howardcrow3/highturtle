What Golden does

  • Statements in, books out

    Import the CSV or OFX files from your bank and card accounts. Golden files each statement, sorts the transactions into categories and remembers how you corrected them.

  • Cash flow at any scale

    See what came in and went out by week, month, quarter or year, for each person or the whole household.

  • A retirement plan you can test

    Golden projects your money forward with federal and state tax, and runs thousands of possible market outcomes to show how likely the plan is to last.

  • Ask “what if”

    Type a question such as “what if we both retire at 62” and see the effect on the plan before anything changes.

  • Two people, one plan

    Every account and income belongs to one of you or to both, with its own retirement age and Social Security age.

  • Locked on your computer

    The database is encrypted, and its key is kept in your computer's keychain. Golden never moves money or places trades.
