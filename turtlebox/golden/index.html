What Golden does

Your books

  • Statements in, books out

    Import CSV, OFX or text PDF statements one at a time, a whole folder at once, or from a folder Golden watches. Each import is checked against the closing balance the statement prints.

  • Categories that learn

    Fix a category once and Golden remembers that merchant. The built-in model suggests categories for lines it hasn't seen, and transfers between your own accounts never count as spending.

  • Ask in plain words

    Ask “what did we spend on groceries last quarter?” and get an answer taken only from your own books.

  • Month close

    Check a finished month, read a short summary, and lock it so nothing changes by accident. Reopen it if you need to.

  • Paystubs and withholding

    Paste a paystub to add pay to the plan, and check whether enough tax is being taken out of each paycheck.

Spending and saving

  • Where the money goes

    Budgets, cash flow by week, month, quarter or year, and a flow chart of money in and out with a slider for each year.

  • What repeats

    Golden finds your subscriptions, bills and paychecks, adds up what they cost in a year, and shows when the next one is due.

  • What stands out

    Plain-language notes on last month: what went up, what went down, and anything unusual.

  • Debts and goals

    Compare paying debts off by highest rate or smallest balance, see what extra money saves, and track savings goals against their dates.

  • Net worth over time

    Everything you own and owe, a month-end history, and a 12-month forecast of where you're headed.

Your retirement

  • When you could retire

    The earliest age that meets the success target you choose, for each of you, with milestones such as your FI number, Coast FI, Social Security, Medicare and required withdrawals.

  • The chance it lasts

    Thousands of market paths drawn from historical returns, with a fan chart of good and rough outcomes. Backtest the plan against real past markets.

  • Safe spending

    The most you can spend each year at the comfort level you pick, with spending rules and the order to draw from your accounts.

  • Progress against the plan

    See how your real net worth is tracking against what the plan expected, with check-ins along the way.

  • A score for your plan

    A checklist of what's missing and warnings where the plan may be less accurate than it looks.

Taxes

  • Federal and state income tax

    Including the alternative minimum tax, the SALT cap, investment income tax, the senior deductions, and Social Security taxed by the IRS rules.

  • Roth conversions

    Compare fixed or bracket-filling conversions side by side, including ones sized to stay under the first Medicare surcharge.

  • Medicare and required withdrawals

    Medicare premiums with the two-year income lookback, required minimum distributions, and giving straight from an IRA.

  • Gains and subsidies

    How much gain you can take at 0% tax, and where the health insurance subsidy cliff sits.

  • Self-employed

    Self-employment tax, the QBI deduction, and how much you can put in a Solo 401(k) or SEP IRA.

Big decisions

  • Ask “what if”

    Type “what if we both retire at 62” and see the plan with and without the change. Save scenarios and compare up to three.

  • When to claim Social Security

    Outcomes for each claiming age, and the age where waiting pays off.

  • Home and family

    Rent or buy, paying off the mortgage, downsizing, and children's childcare, college and tax credit.

  • Health care

    Estimates for health care and long-term care costs that you can adjust.

  • Investments and estate

    A check-up of your mix, fees and concentration, plus estate tax, the inherited IRA ten-year rule, early-withdrawal plans and donor-advised fund bunching.

Your data

  • Encrypted on your computer

    The database and a document vault for tax forms and statements are encrypted, with the key in your computer's keychain.

  • Private by default

    A Privacy page lists every outside service and what it would send. Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn it on.

  • Hide amounts

    One click hides dollar amounts when you share your screen.

  • Reports and plan files

    Save a printable plan report with CSV and JSON data, or share your plan as a file.

  • AI that stays home

    The built-in AI model runs on your computer. AI assistants you connect get read-only access, so they can look at your plan but never change it.

  • Two people, one plan

    Every account and income belongs to one of you or to both, including pensions, employer match and HSA.
