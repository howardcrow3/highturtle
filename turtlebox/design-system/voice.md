# Voice

Turtlebox sounds like a capable colleague describing a tool plainly. It is calm, specific and unhurried. It never hypes and never creates urgency.

## Rules

- Say what the app does and who it is for in the first sentence. "Paste a product spec and get a list of gaps and open questions before it goes to engineering."
- Use plain verbs and active voice. A button says what happens: "Email me the download". Keep the same name through a flow: if the button says "Email me", the confirmation says "Sent".
- Write in sentence case. "See the apps", not "See The Apps". The stylesheet capitalizes labels and buttons for you.
- Be specific over clever. Prefer a number or an example to an adjective.
- Say what is true now. Statuses are literal: "Planned", "In testing", "Live". Do not imply something is available before it is. If a feature does not exist, say "not yet".
- Use "we" for the studio and "you" for the reader.
- Keep sentences short. Avoid asides set off by dashes, "not X but Y" framing, colon-then-reveal sentences and stock phrases.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, and none of: amazing, awesome, powerful, revolutionary, best.
- Errors say what went wrong and what to do, without apology.
- The name is Turtlebox: one word, capital T, lowercase b. Never "Turtle Box" or "TurtleBox". The domain is written in lowercase: turtlebox.studio. The sister studio is High Turtle, two words.

## Vocabulary

- Say "app" for a Turtlebox product and "task" for the one job it does.
- Say "download" for the installable file and "open" for an app that runs in the browser.

## Real examples

- Headline: "Small AI apps that each do one job."
- Lede: "Turtlebox builds AI apps that run in your browser. Each one is built for a single task, so you can open it, use it and get on with your day."
- Primary action: "See the apps"
- Secondary action: "Visit High Turtle"
- Statuses: "Planned", "In testing", "Live"
- Form label: "Email address". Placeholder: "you@company.com".
- Download action: "Email me the download"
- Form note: "We email you a download link. You also join the High Turtle list for release news and can unsubscribe any time."
- Empty catalog: "The first apps are in the works."
- Footer: "Turtlebox builds small AI apps."
