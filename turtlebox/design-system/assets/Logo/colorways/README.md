Logo colorways drawn from the palette. Only pairs that hold 4.5:1 are included, and each file carries its own ground and a single ink.

- `charcoal-on-ivory` and `charcoal-on-warm-white` are the default light looks (15.0:1 and 14.0:1).
- `ivory-on-charcoal` is the default dark look (15.0:1).
- `charcoal-on-stone` (8.8:1) and `charcoal-on-gold` (7.7:1) are for covers, social posts and packaging where a warm field is wanted.
- `stone-on-charcoal` (8.8:1) is a quieter dark look. `gold-on-charcoal` (7.7:1) is the accent look: use it sparingly, on a mark or a social avatar, never for running text.
- Gold on ivory, ash grounds and any other pair fail contrast and are not supplied.
- `mark-*` files are 512px squares with the mark at 62%. `horizontal-*` files are the horizontal lockup with 48px padding.

Regenerate with `python3 build_colorways.py`.
