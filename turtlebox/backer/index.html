What Backer does

  • Scheduled or on demand

    Set a schedule and forget it, or back up new photos and videos yourself when you want to.

  • Big libraries

    Handles 100,000+ files, so years of photos and video footage are fine.

  • Verify

    Compares what is on the NAS with your originals, so you know every photo and video was copied intact.

  • Asks before deleting

    Backer never removes a photo or video from the NAS without asking you first.

  • Optional AI assistant integration

    A local MCP server lets an AI assistant work with Backer. It is off by default.

  • Stays out of the way

    Lives in the menu bar on a Mac and in the system tray on Windows.

Install

macOS

Open the DMG and drag Backer to Applications. The app is signed and notarized.

Windows

Run the installer. It is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say “unknown publisher”. Click More info, then Run anyway.

After you buy

Your license key appears on the confirmation page and arrives by email.

  1. In Backer, open Settings, then License.
  2. Paste the key.
  3. Click Activate.

Keys are tied to the email address you bought with. If something does not work, report an issue or write to info@turtlebox.studio.

Privacy. Backer sends nothing anywhere except update checks to our server and the purchase page. There is no telemetry. This website sets no cookies and has no tracking scripts.
