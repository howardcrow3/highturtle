What Backer does

Backing up

  • To a NAS you own

    Copies to a shared folder on a Synology or any other NAS over SMB or NFS. No cloud and no account.

  • On a schedule or on demand

    Daily, weekly or every few hours. If your computer was asleep, Backer catches up when it wakes.

  • Big libraries

    Handles 100,000+ files and copies only what is new or changed, so later runs are quick.

  • Only what you want

    Leave out files over a size limit, and skip system junk such as thumbnails and temporary files.

Keeping copies safe

  • Checked as it copies

    Each file is written to a temporary name, checked against your original, and only then given its real name.

  • Never deletes without asking

    When you delete something on your computer, Backer lists it under Deleted files and waits for you to decide.

  • The right place every time

    Backer marks the backup folder on the NAS, so it won't write into the wrong share if a drive changes.

  • Verify

    Compares what is on the NAS with your originals, so you know every photo and video arrived intact.

Day to day

  • Clear run summaries

    See what was copied, what was skipped and why, for each folder. Export the log as a CSV file.

  • Browse and restore

    Find a file in your backup and copy it back. Restore keeps working even after the trial ends.

  • Stays out of the way

    Lives in the menu bar on a Mac and in the system tray on Windows.

  • Optional AI assistant integration

    A local MCP server lets an AI assistant you approve check on Backer. It is off by default.

Install

macOS

Open the DMG and drag Backer to Applications. The app is signed and notarized.

Windows

Run the installer. It is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say “unknown publisher”. Click More info, then Run anyway.

After you buy

Your license key appears on the confirmation page and arrives by email.

  1. In Backer, open Settings, then License.
  2. Paste the key.
  3. Click Activate.

Keys are tied to the email address you bought with. If something does not work, report an issue or write to info@turtlebox.studio.
