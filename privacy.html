Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

This notice covers highturtle.com, turtlebox.studio, and the High Turtle and Turtlebox products, including the Backer app. We collect as little as we can.

Who is responsible

High Turtle LLC, trading as High Turtle and Turtlebox, of 15790 Redmond Way #1171, Redmond, WA 98052, USA, is the controller of your personal data. Privacy questions and requests: hello@highturtle.com or info@turtlebox.studio.

What we collect, why, and for how long

  • Email list. Your email address and when you confirmed it, so we can send news about High Turtle and Turtlebox releases. Legal basis: your consent. Kept until you unsubscribe. A signup you never confirm is deleted after 48 hours. After you unsubscribe, your address stays on a suppression list only so we never email you again.
  • App downloads. Your email address and the app you asked for, so we can email you a download link. Asking for a download also adds you to the email list, as the form says. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Purchases. Paddle, our Merchant of Record, collects your name, email, billing address, country and payment details at checkout and shares the order details with us (name, email, country, what you bought and your license key). We use them to deliver your purchase, give support and keep business records. Legal basis: to perform our contract with you, and our legal obligations for tax and accounting. Kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We never see your full card details.
  • Issue reports. What you type into the Backer report form: a summary, details, your computer type, Backer version, NAS model, and your email if you add one so we can reply. We use it to fix problems and answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in supporting and improving our products. Kept while the issue is open and for up to 2 years after, so we can spot repeat problems.
  • Emails you send us. Your message and address, to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying. Kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • The Backer app. Backer checks our server for updates. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and the app's version. Backer sends no telemetry, and your files and backups never leave your computer and NAS.
  • Your account. If you sign in on turtlebox.studio with Google, Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture. We use them to show your orders and license keys and let you manage billing. Legal basis: to perform our contract with you. They are kept only in a sign-in cookie on your device, which expires after 7 days or when you sign out.
  • Website visits. Our sites use no analytics or tracking scripts, and set no cookies unless you sign in to your account. Our hosting provider handles your IP address to deliver pages and may keep short-lived logs for security.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record, which processes orders, payments, taxes and refunds. Paddle uses your data as described in its privacy notice.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google, which confirms who you are under its privacy policy.
  • Service providers that run things for us: Amazon Web Services (hosting, email sending and storage, in the United States), GitHub (our private issue tracker, for issue reports) and MXroute (our email inbox).
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, when needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

International transfers

Our service providers store data in the United States. Where data from the UK or the European Economic Area is transferred, we rely on an adequacy decision or the standard contractual clauses (or the UK addendum) that these providers offer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time, for example with the unsubscribe link in every email. Write to us to use any of these rights and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. California and other US state residents have similar rights, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls and providers with strong security practices, and we keep only what we need.

Cookies

Our own pages set no cookies unless you sign in to your Turtlebox account. Signing in sets one strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days; signing out removes it. Paddle's checkout may use cookies it needs to process your payment safely; those are covered by Paddle's privacy notice.

Children

Our products are not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version.
