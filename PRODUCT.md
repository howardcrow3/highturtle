## Platform

web

## Users

highturtle.com is the public face of High Turtle LLC. In order of priority, its visitors are:

1. **Payment providers and partners** checking that High Turtle LLC is a real business. They look for a legal name, location, contact details, terms, refund and privacy policies. Paddle reviewed the site on this basis.
2. **Customers of a portfolio company** who want to know who stands behind the product they bought or are considering.
3. **People who may work with High Turtle**, plus anyone who wants launch news through the email list.

The site is not a pitch to outside investors, even though the portfolio list follows a VC style.

## Product Purpose

High Turtle LLC is a holding company for small, focused AI companies. It starts them, keeps them for the long term and gives them shared infrastructure. The site shows that the company exists and is legitimate, lists the portfolio, and links each company to its own site. It succeeds when a reviewer, customer or partner leaves sure of who runs the companies and how to reach them.

## Positioning

High Turtle provides the shared infrastructure: legal, payments, hosting, design, email and accounts are set up once and used by every company. Each portfolio company keeps its own offering, brand, products and terms. High Turtle is the foundation underneath, not a parent brand placed over the companies.

## Operating Context

- Portfolio (as of 2026-11-16): Turtlebox (operating, turtlebox.studio), Montoya.ai, Murderbot.ai and Haven AI (early stage, each with its own site), and Saltine.ai, Born Normal and Mortal Jellyfish (stealth, no site). New companies are added as rows in the portfolio list in `index.html`.
- Turtlebox is the operating sister brand in this repo (`turtlebox/`). It sells small AI apps and the Backer desktop app through Paddle. It has its own voice (`turtlebox/design-system/voice.md`) and shares the design system with highturtle.com.
- Legal pages (terms, refunds, privacy, contact) are generated by `legal/build.py` and are part of what payment providers review.
- The site is a static site with no build step for pages, served from S3 and CloudFront. Merging to `main` deploys it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home (hero, portfolio, approach, launch-news signup, company details), legal pages, contact, and the signup confirmation and error pages.
- The launch-news signup posts to `/api/subscribe` and works without JavaScript.
- highturtle.com is the primary domain. highturtle.ai serves the same site. Email stays @highturtle.com.
- Montoya.ai is not a trading name of High Turtle LLC unless Howard confirms it. Trading names are High Turtle and Turtlebox.

## Brand Commitments

- **Users' intellectual property and privacy are sacred.** Customers' work, data and ideas belong to them. This principle binds High Turtle and every portfolio company. Future work must not weaken it. Any specific public promise based on it, such as how data is used with AI models, needs Howard's confirmation and must match the privacy policy before it goes on a page.
- Keep the founder's personal name off the sites.
- Write plainly and say only what is true now, in the voice described in `turtlebox/design-system/voice.md`: calm, specific, no hype, no urgency.
- Describe stealth companies neutrally until Howard supplies a one-liner.
- The name is High Turtle (two words). The logo is the gold turtle mark in `assets/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real company facts: legal name, street address (15790 Redmond Way #1171, Redmond, WA 98052, USA), contact address and the portfolio list.
- Logo, mark and social image in `assets/`.
- There are no testimonials, customer counts, revenue, metrics or press. Do not invent any of them.

## Product Principles

1. Prove legitimacy first. A reviewer should find the legal name, contact and policies without searching.
2. Each company keeps its own offering and brand. High Turtle stays in the background as shared infrastructure.
3. Protect the customer's intellectual property and privacy in every product and every claim.
4. Built to last. Only say what is true now, and nothing that would need walking back.
