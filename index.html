What we make

  • Claude skills

    Drafted, in testing

    Reusable instructions that teach Claude how to do one product-making task well: frame a problem, prioritize a backlog, design an experiment. The first pack runs from discovery to sunset, and each skill is tested on a real product problem before it ships.

  • Playbooks and templates

    Planned

    Short PDF guides and working files for decisions product teams keep making again: scoring models, sizing models, risk registers and OKR tables, with live formulas where they help.

  • Micro-tools

    Planned

    Small tools that do one product job, like sizing an opportunity or checking a spec for gaps.
