Our approach

  • Small and focused

    Each company does one thing for one kind of customer. Narrow products are easier to make good and easier to trust.

  • Built to last

    We hold our companies for the long term and grow them steadily, so customers can count on the products they buy still being here.

  • One shared foundation

    Legal, payments, hosting, design and email are set up once at High Turtle, so each company can spend its time on its product.
